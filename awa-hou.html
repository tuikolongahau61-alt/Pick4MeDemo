What is 'awa?
A quick guide to kava — what it is, how it feels, and how to enjoy it.
🌿
What is 'awa (kava)?
'Awa is a traditional drink made from the root of the Piper methysticum plant — a relative of black pepper. People across the South Pacific have shared it for over 3,000 years. It tastes earthy and gently numbs the tongue.
😌
How it makes you feel
'Awa brings a calm, relaxed, sociable feeling — a natural way to unwind without alcohol. Many find it eases stress, loosens conversation, and helps them settle in. No hangover, and it won't impair you the way alcohol does.
🥥
Fresh vs. Dry
Fresh Hawaiian 'awa is grown on the Big Island and never dried — milder and smoother, great for first-timers. Dry kava comes from across the South Pacific and is dried for a stronger mix. Add coconut water (niu) to round out either one.
🪵
'Apu vs. Kānoa
An 'apu is your own 8 oz cup. A kānoa is a shared bowl — the traditional way to drink 'awa together. Order a kānoa for the table and keep everyone's shells full.
🥥
How to drink it
Served in a coconut shell. Say 'ōkole maluna (bottoms up) and drink your 'apu in one go. Your tongue may tingle — that's the 'awa. Chase with a little water or pineapple, then sink into the calm.
🌙
What to expect
Effects come on gently, usually within 15–20 minutes. Start with one and go slow — especially your first time. Please don't mix 'awa with alcohol, and arrange a safe ride if you've had a few. Pregnant or on medication? Check with your doctor first.
